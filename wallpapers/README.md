# Bootsy wallpapers

Original artwork generated for bootsy-linux with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool. The source prompts intentionally request original imagery
without logos, text, people, or imitation of a named artist's style.

- `bootsy-aurora.webp` — cobalt aurora over a mountain lake
- `bootsy-rock-desert.webp` — weathered high-desert mesas after rain
- `bootsy-bamboo-grove.webp` — a misty, sunlit bamboo forest
- `bootsy-rice-terraces.webp` — emerald rice paddies across mountain hills
- `bootsy-alpine-mountains.webp` — snow peaks above a turquoise glacial lake
- `bootsy-white-beach.webp` — a pristine white-sand tropical cove
- `bootsy-tropical-sea.webp` — blue reef channels and uninhabited islets
- `bootsy-sand-dunes.webp` — minimalist wind-sculpted dunes at sunrise
- `bootsy-sunflower-fields.webp` — rolling golden fields beneath blue hills
- `bootsy-mineral-dunes.webp` — crystalline formations in a warm desert
- `bootsy-zircon-flow.webp` — dark abstract mineral ribbons (default)

All images are 1672×941 WebP at quality 92 and are installed under
`/usr/share/backgrounds/bootsy-linux`.
